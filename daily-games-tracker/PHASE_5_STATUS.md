# Phase 5 status

Phase 5A and 5B source, tests, and pinned build contracts are maintained in
the public candidate. Bounded user-reported native behavior and later build
identities remain historical evidence; exact native acceptance, rollback,
operational signing, and reproducibility retain their Phase 9 dispositions.

## Current state

- Credential-free Cherri sources, inspectors, and signing guards are present
  under `shortcut/cherri-migration/`.
- The website's Login with Device flow is implemented with same-origin,
  one-use browser challenges and active-session/revocation checks.
- The Phase 4 functional pilot remains accepted with its explicit exceptions.
- Saved-result import CPU remains open monitoring, and new-save CPU remains
  unmeasured.
- Physical device/native checks, production configuration, rollout, and
  distribution remain future launch tasks.

Private device codes, configured Shortcut files, player captures/results,
signing request/response contents, and local operational evidence are excluded
from the public tree. See [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md) and
[PHASE_9_LAUNCH_GATES.md](PHASE_9_LAUNCH_GATES.md) for the authoritative
current dispositions.
